20 Questions to Ask Movers in the UAE Before You Accept a Quote

A practical list of questions to send UAE moving companies by email or WhatsApp, grouped by price, service, logistics and cover, so you can choose with confidence.

The best way to understand a moving quote is to ask good questions about it. Most reputable movers are happy to answer, and the answers tell you as much about the company as they do about the price. Here are twenty questions worth sending, grouped so you can copy them into a message. You can ask them in writing, which gives you a record to refer back to.

About the price

  1. Is this a fixed price or an estimate? If it is an estimate, what could change it?
  2. Does the price include VAT?
  3. What is the deposit, when is the balance due and which payment methods do you accept?
  4. Are there any surcharges for weekends, evenings, public holidays or long carries?
  5. How long is this quote valid?

These questions make sure the number on the page is the number you will actually pay. A company that answers clearly here is usually clear elsewhere too.

About the service

  1. Which items and rooms are included in packing, and which are not?
  2. How many cartons, wardrobe boxes and other materials are included?
  3. Will you dismantle and reassemble beds, wardrobes and other furniture?
  4. Is unpacking included, and does that mean placing boxes or fully unpacking?
  5. Do you offer handyman services such as TV mounting or curtain installation, and how are they charged?

Service questions catch the gaps where cheap quotes often hide their costs. Comparing the answers side by side makes it obvious which quotes include more.

About logistics

  1. How many crew members and trucks will you send, and will there be a supervisor?
  2. How long do you expect the move to take?
  3. What happens if the job takes longer than planned?
  4. Will you help with building move permits or lift bookings, or is that my responsibility?
  5. What are your working hours, and can you work within my building's moving hours?

Logistics questions help you plan the day. In many UAE towers, moves must happen within a booked lift slot, so knowing the crew size and expected duration matters.

About cover and trust

  1. What liability or insurance is included, and what is the limit?
  2. How do I report damage, and how long do I have to do so?
  3. Are self-packed boxes covered?
  4. Can you share your trade licence or company registration details?
  5. Can you put everything we have agreed into a written quote or confirmation?

These last questions protect you if something goes wrong. A company that hesitates to put details in writing or to share its licence is worth thinking twice about.

How to use these questions

You do not need to send all twenty to every mover at once. A practical approach is to send your move details first, then follow up with the questions the quote does not already answer. Keep the replies in one place, such as a spreadsheet or notes app, with a column for each company.

If you prefer not to take phone calls, ask for everything by email or WhatsApp text. Many UAE movers are happy to work this way, and written answers are easier to compare.

Reading the answers

Good answers are specific. "Yes, four movers, one supervisor, one closed truck, around five hours" is more useful than "don't worry, we will manage". When a company gives vague answers to simple questions, it may be equally vague on moving day.

Also notice tone. Are they patient with your questions? Do they explain their assumptions? Do they push you to pay a deposit immediately? These signals often predict how the company will handle problems.

Comparing companies

Once you have answers from three or four movers, compare them on what matters most to you: price, service level, reliability and cover. The cheapest is not always the best value if it excludes packing materials or has very limited liability. The most expensive is not always the best if you do not need the extras it includes.

If you would rather start with a shortlist, you can request mover quotes once and receive written replies from a few vetted companies, then send these questions to the ones you like.

Before moving day

After choosing a mover, confirm:

  • The date and arrival time
  • The crew size and truck details
  • The full scope of work
  • The price, deposit and balance terms
  • Contact details for the supervisor on the day

For official information on residential moves, tenancies and services across the emirates, the UAE government portal is a reliable reference.

A note for first-time movers in the UAE

If this is your first move in the country, you may not know which questions matter most locally. Building permits, lift bookings and summer heat are the three things newcomers most often underestimate. Ask about all three, and give yourself a few extra days in the plan.

Questions movers may ask you

Expect a good mover to ask questions too: your floor levels, lift access, parking distance, bulky items, preferred date and whether you need packing. Have these answers ready in one short message. The faster and more complete your answers, the more accurate and comparable the quotes you receive will be.

Summary

Twenty questions may sound like a lot, but most movers answer them in a few lines. The answers turn a vague price into a clear agreement and help you pick a company you can trust with your home. Ask in writing, compare carefully and confirm everything before moving day.
